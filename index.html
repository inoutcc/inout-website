FAQ

Common questions.

Is in/out really free?

Yes — core calorie and macro tracking is completely free with no time limit. You get 5 AI food logs per day on the free plan. Pro ($2 one-time) and AI subscription ($4/mo) are optional upgrades.

How accurate is the AI food logging?

Very accurate for common foods and meals. The AI uses contextual understanding — "large bowl of pasta bolognese" gives you a reasonable estimate based on standard serving sizes. For precise tracking, you can adjust portions after logging.

Does it work with my smartwatch or fitness tracker?

Yes — in/out integrates with Android Health Connect, which can read supported data from apps like Fitbit, Samsung Health, Garmin Connect, Polar Flow, Oura, Whoop, and more. in/out currently requests read-only access for steps, heart rate, weight, and active calories burned.

Can I cancel the AI subscription anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel any time before the 7-day trial ends and you won't be charged. Manage your subscription directly through Google Play.

Is my health data private and secure?

Yes. All data is stored securely with row-level security. AI requests are proxied through our backend — no raw health data is ever sent to AI providers. You can delete your account and all data at any time from Settings.

Is in/out available on iOS?

Currently Android only. iOS is on the roadmap — follow us for updates.
